A classic cold-weather drink. Pairs nicely with comfortable slippers and a crackling fire on a winter evening.

* 1 oz. honey (clover is fine)
* 1 oz. lemon juice (fresh squeezed is best)
* 1 oz. bourbon or whiskey
* 8 oz. hot water

Proportions may be varied to taste.
